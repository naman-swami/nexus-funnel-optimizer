# Explainability — nexus-funnel-optimizer

## Decision Reasoning
Nexus analyzes pipeline velocity by calculating statistical win rates across cohort attributes, measuring buying intent velocity, and aligning messaging with verified organizational pain points.

## Data Sources and Inputs Used
CRM pipeline stages (Salesforce/HubSpot), intent telemetry (G2, Clearbit), product analytics (Mixpanel), and earnings transcripts.

## Confidence Scoring Methodology
Before returning a final recommendation or analysis, nexus-funnel-optimizer assigns an internal confidence score (0–100%) based on:
1. **Source Grounding**: High (90–100%) when corroborated by primary authoritative standards and deterministic checks.
2. **Structural Completeness**: Moderate (75–89%) when operating on partial context or heuristic inferences.
3. If confidence falls below 85%, nexus-funnel-optimizer will explicitly prepend a disclaimer to the user.

## Source Attribution Protocol
When relying on specific named standards, statutory codes, or operational benchmarks, nexus-funnel-optimizer explicitly cites the governing framework or canonical specification rather than presenting deductions as ungrounded truths.

## Bias Awareness
nexus-funnel-optimizer actively accounts for domain-specific operational biases:
- **Baseline Skew**: Avoids over-indexing on standard common scenarios at the expense of rare edge cases.
- **Reporting Disparity**: Recognizes that historical telemetry and training data may underrepresent frontier or non-standard architectures.
- **Jurisdictional & Demographic Neutrality**: Strives to maintain universal, objective evaluation standards across varying environments.

## Limitation Taxonomy per Domain
- Price Negotiation: Cannot offer unapproved bespoke pricing discounts outside standard board rate cards.
- Legal Commitments: Outreach communications carry no binding contractual promises regarding delivery schedules.
- Data Privacy: Strictly complies with CAN-SPAM and GDPR, refusing unverified third-party scraping.
- Subjective Sentiment: Cannot account for off-the-record executive relationships or undisclosed corporate restructurings.

## Uncertainty Quantification Approach
When firmographic data is incomplete (e.g., stealth startups), Nexus assigns a wide variance margin, flags the lead for manual SDR qualification, and avoids making premature sales projections.
