# SaaS Financial Unit Economics & Growth Funnel Standards

## 1. Venture Financial Guidelines & Standards
Nexus evaluates B2B SaaS growth metrics in alignment with **Bessemer Venture Partners (BVP) State of the Cloud** and **OpenView Product-Led Growth Benchmarks**.

---

## 2. Core SaaS Unit Economics Formulations

### A. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Reflects fully-loaded marketing and sales expenditures required to win a single new customer:

$$\text{CAC} = \frac{\text{S\&M Headcount} + \text{Paid Ad Spend} + \text{Tooling} + \text{Commissions}}{\text{New Logo Customers Acquired}}$$

### B. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
Calculates projected gross profit contribution over the average customer tenure:

$$\text{LTV} = \frac{\text{Average Revenue Per Account (ARPA)} \times \text{Gross Margin (\%)}}{\text{Annual Logo Churn Rate (\%)}}$$

### C. LTV to CAC Ratio & Benchmarks:
- **$\text{LTV} / \text{CAC} < 1.0\times$**: Value destruction; company loses capital on every acquired customer.
- **$1.0\times \le \text{LTV} / \text{CAC} < 3.0\times$**: Sub-optimal growth efficiency; long cash payback.
- **$\ge 3.0\times$**: **Industry Gold Standard**; healthy sustainable unit economics.
- **$> 5.0\times$**: Under-investing in acquisition; business should accelerate marketing spend.

### D. CAC Payback Period (Months)
$$\text{CAC Payback} = \frac{\text{CAC}}{\text{ARPA} \times \text{Gross Margin (\%)}}$$
*Target*: $\le 12\text{ months}$ for self-serve/SMB SaaS; $\le 18\text{ months}$ for enterprise multi-year contracts.

## 3. Funnel Attribution & Stage Conversion Waterfall
The funnel engine tracks progression through 5 deterministic stages:
1. **Visitor $\rightarrow$ Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL)**: Baseline conversion benchmark $3.5\% - 5.0\%$.
2. **MQL $\rightarrow$ Sales Qualified Lead (SQL)**: Acceptance benchmark $25\% - 35\%$.
3. **SQL $\rightarrow$ Opportunity**: Qualification benchmark $40\% - 50\%$.
4. **Opportunity $\rightarrow$ Closed Won**: Win-rate benchmark $20\% - 25\%$.
